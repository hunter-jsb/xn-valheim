Map commands

Type these in the in-game chat (press Enter to open chat).

Pins

!pindrop an unnamed dot where you stand
!pin <text>drop a dot with a label
!pin <type> <text>a typed pin, e.g. !pin house Base
!undoPinremove your most recent pin
!deletePin <text>remove the pin matching that text
!deletePinwith no text, removes your most recent unnamed pin

Layers

Each row in the sidebar's Layers list is a switch, remembered by this browser. Zoomed right in, builds turn from roofs into a floor plan; hide floor plan when close to keep the roofs. Portal lines join linked pairs, and a grave is gear still lying there.

Show yourself on the map

Press M in game and turn on visible to other players. Without it you still show in the online list, but never with a location.

Good to know

Players and pins refresh every few seconds; builds and the explored mask every minute while someone has the map open.
