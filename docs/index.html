Map commands

Type these in the in-game chat (press Enter to open chat).

Pins

!pindrop an unnamed dot where you stand !pin <text>drop a dot with a label !pin <type> <text>drop a typed pin, e.g. !pin house Base !undoPinremove your most recent pin !deletePin <text>remove the pin matching that text
Types: dot · fire · mine · house · cave

Show yourself on the map

Press M in game and turn on visible to other players. Without it you still show in the online list, but never with a location.

Good to know

Pins, players and chat here refresh every few minutes. The live map updates in real time.
